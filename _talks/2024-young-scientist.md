Through comparative characterization of additive and conventionally manufactured (AM and CM) 8620 steel microstructures, insights to improve the performance of AM parts can be obtained. This seminar focuses on comparative characterization of parts produced through AM and CM for the steel alloy 8620, a low carbon steel, and two graphene application methods. The graphene coatings are prepared by exfoliation of graphene flakes, or by direct chemical vapor deposition growth of graphene on the steel surfaces. Characterization results include optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and electrochemical testing. Successful application of graphene coatings to surfaces, and the anti-corrosive performance of coatings is dependent on the surface morphology and the microstructure of the 8620 steel samples.
