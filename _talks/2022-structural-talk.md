Mono- and few-layer exfoliated graphene (EG) sheets can be produced by a “Thermal Expansion--Liquid Exfoliation” process. The entire exfoliation process does not involve a stabilizer or chemical modifications. Thus, the approach minimizes the structural defects significantly. These EG sheets show great potential for use as anti-corrosion thin films.
