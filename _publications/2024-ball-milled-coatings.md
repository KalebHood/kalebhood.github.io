**Abstract** 
This work utilizes ball milling (BM) to apply graphene coatings to 316L steel spheres by mechanically exfoliating graphene sheets from an expanded graphite (EG) precursor. BM at different revolutions per minute (RPM) was found to have a direct impact on the quality of graphene coating [1]. This study suggests that low speed milling (210 RPM) results in larger, less defective flakes with lower coverage. Conversely, high speed milling (620 RPM) results in higher coating coverage but with smaller and more damaged graphene flakes. The preferred milling speed for moderate coverage and less damaged graphene flakes was 415 RPM demonstrated by the results presented in this report.
