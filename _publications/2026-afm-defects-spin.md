**Abstract**
Spintronics is a sub-field of electronics where the degree of freedom is the spin of an electron rather than its charge [1]. Spintroniclogic devices have been proposed as successors to their charge-based counterparts due to their ability to operate at higher frequen-cies while consuming less power. Graphene is a popular channel material for transporting spin polarized currents due to its abilityto preserve spin polarization over large distances. Recent advancements in chemical vapor deposition (CVD) grown graphenehave resulted in commercially available graphene on SiO2 wafers of up to 150 mm diameter. However, commercially availablegraphene contains defects arising from the CVD growth process such as grain boundaries and multilayer regions, and roll-to-rolltransfer results in nanoscale wrinkles, necessitating a method for studying the impact of these defects on spin transport.
