**Abstract**
This study demonstrates the increased absorbance of copper powder performed by texturing the surfaces through annealing. Copper powder has low optical absorbance in the visible and near-infrared (NIR) range, requiring higher laser power for melting in laser-based metal additive manufacturing processes [1]. One study found that chemical etching could be used to texture the copper powder surfaces, resulting in increased optical absorbance at long wavelengths [2]. In comparison, this study found that recrystallization caused by annealing resulted in the formation of crevices, pores, and a highly textured surface which increased the optical absorbance by up to 117% in the NIR range.
