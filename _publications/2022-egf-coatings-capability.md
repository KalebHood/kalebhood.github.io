**Abstract**
Graphene Oxide (GO) has been used with surfactants1 to create conformal coatings or with polymers to
create diffusion barriers3, and CVD graphene (G) films have shown anti-corrosive properties2 after a
difficult transfer process. The study of exfoliated graphene flakes (EGFs) and water to create a film has
not been reported. EGFs have advantages with an intact crystal lattice, However, using EGFs to create a
conformal graphene thin film coating, by self-assembling without surfactant has not been studied.
