A workshop designed to help summer MSURE (Maseeh Summer Undergraduate Research Experience) interns develop their science communication skills. The highly interactive workshop broke down how to meet your audience where they are at, sift through jargon while maintaining technical accuracy, how to develop exciting hooks for your research topic, and a little bit of acting skills to enhance your scientific storytelling.
