The course is designed to introduce the theoretical concepts of
transmission electron microscopy (TEM) and its practical applications. Specifically, the course
will enable students to understand electron optics in relation to TEM and energy dispersive X-ray (EDX)
microanalysis, gain knowledge of using TEM and EDX techniques for materials characterization at
the atomic scale, become competent in data analysis and image interpretation, including bright field,
dark field, and phase contrast TEM images, as well as EDX spectra, enhance problem-solving skills by combining theoretical knowledge with selected
case studies of TEM/EDX techniques for technological applications, and gain hands-on experience with a TEM simulator through weekly demonstrations and practice sessions.
