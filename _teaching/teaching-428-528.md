This course is designed to introduce the theoretical and practical concepts of SEM and spectroscopy techniques. Topics studied will include electron optical principles of SEM, specimen preparation, SEM imaging and result interpretation. The spectroscopy of microanalysis covers qualitative and quantitative chemical analysis of materials enabled by the SEM and its analytical attachments. Lectures will incorporate the basic design of the SEM, the image formation mechanism, and EDX spectroscopy with their practical applications to materials and device characterizations. The lectures and the lab sessions are integrated to enhance the learning experience of these state-of-the-art materials science and engineering techniques.
