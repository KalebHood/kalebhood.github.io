Hi! Welcome to the landing page for my website. 

I'm a material science engineer, with a background in chemistry and biological science, pursuing a PhD in mechanical engineering. In essence, material science engineers try to understand the natural and human designed materials which make up the world around us, and work to improve them for a safer and better world. Broadly, my research is in the sub-field of nanomaterial synthesis, characterization and applications, especially for surface engineering and semiconductors. My Research
======

My research builds on my background in chemistry, material science and mechanical engineering. My main focus of my dissertation work is on the development of graphene and carbon nanotube coatings for tailored steel surface properties. The graphene coatings improve the durability of the steel by imparting corrosion resistance, and manipulating the wettability, tribology, chemical potential and conductivity of the steel surfaces. I use two methods to coat steels with graphene: exfoliating graphene sheets which arrange into a self-assembled film, and directly growing graphene on the steel surfaces using chemical vapor deposition. The direct growth method uses a novel technique to manipulate the phase of steel surfaces, utilizing iron carbide as an intermediate phase. 

I also collaborate in cross-disciplinary research including:
- Spintronic devices
- Organic single crystal surface reactions
- Carbohydrate neuromorphic reRAM devices
- Additive manufactured composites
- Microscopy and Spectroscopy for materials characterization
